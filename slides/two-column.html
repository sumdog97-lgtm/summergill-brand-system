Stage 01 / Enquiry

Every enquiry gets a straight answer, same day.

Qualify in three questions

  • What does the business do, and where?
  • What's the website situation now?
  • What do they want more of?

Channel discipline

WhatsApp-first, always. Initial enquiry stays in writing. Offer a call once scope is clear, never as the opening move.
