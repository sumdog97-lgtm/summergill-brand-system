The Summergill CTA button — square, cobalt, semibold; use primary for the WhatsApp action and outline for secondary navigation.

```jsx
<Button icon="whatsapp">WhatsApp us about yours</Button>
<Button variant="outline" iconRight="arrow">See our work</Button>
<Button size="sm" icon="whatsapp">WhatsApp us</Button>
<Button size="md" fullWidth icon="whatsapp">Claim a build slot</Button>
```

- `variant`: primary | outline | outline-dark (on ink sections).
- No rounded corners, no shadows. Hover is a colour change only.
- Copy: verb-first, conversational ("WhatsApp us", "Send it over WhatsApp").
