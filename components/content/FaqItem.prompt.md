FAQ accordion row — use for question/answer lists.

```jsx
<FaqList items={[{ q: 'Do I actually own my domain?', a: 'Yes — it’s registered to you from day one.' }]} />
<FaqItem question="What if I want to leave?" defaultOpen>After the first 12 months…</FaqItem>
```

Questions are written in the customer's voice ("Do I actually…", "My business is a bit different…").
