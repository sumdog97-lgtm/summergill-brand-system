Standard section opener: numbered mono eyebrow, bold display h2, ink/70 lead — use at the top of every section.

```jsx
<SectionHeader eyebrow="01 / The problem" title="Good businesses lose work to worse ones with better websites." lead="You’re brilliant at what you do. But online, nobody can tell." />
<SectionHeader tone="dark" eyebrow="03 / Pricing" title="One clear price. Everything included." />
```

Headlines are short declarative sentences ending in a full stop.
